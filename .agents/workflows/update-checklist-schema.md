Please update `lib/data/default-tasks.ts` to reflect the new 3-Tier Task Architecture. All tasks must be categorized under one of the following priority tiers, and UI badges should reflect these urgency levels.

**Tier 1: Mandatory (Legal & Settlement Survival)**
*UI Badge Color: Red/Critical*
*   **Immigration:** Print Port of Entry (POE) Letter & Letter of Acceptance (LOA).
*   **Financial Compliance:** Fund the $23,448 CAD Student GIC (IRCC Requirement).
*   **Identity:** Obtain Social Insurance Number (SIN) at Kitchener Service Canada (Required to work legally).
*   **Health:** Register for UHIP (University Health Insurance Plan).
*   **Connectivity:** Activate a Canadian eSIM before boarding the flight (so data works at Pearson Airport).

**Tier 2: High-Leverage Essentials (Financial & Logistics)**
*UI Badge Color: Blue/Essential*
*   **Banking:** Open a zero-fee Canadian student checking account (e.g., Scotiabank StartRight, CIBC Smart, TD, Simplii). 
*   **Transit:** Set up the WatCard for free access to Grand River Transit (GRT) and the ION LRT.
*   **Credit Building:** Apply for a beginner Canadian credit card (crucial for future renting).
*   **Housing:** Review the Ontario Standard Lease agreement and verify tenant rights.

**Tier 3: The "Hacks" (Optional Perks)**
*UI Badge Color: Emerald/Perk*
*   **Discounts:** Secure an SPC (Student Price Card) for retail discounts.
*   **Travel:** Book the GO Transit student discount fare from Pearson (YYZ) to Waterloo.
*   **Groceries:** Download the PC Optimum app (for grocery points).
